Your browser reads the PDF itself — the file is never uploaded. Only the text it contains is sent to our extraction service, where an AI model reads it into fields. Neither the file nor its text is stored, and only the fields you approve on the next screen are kept. If the service can't be reached, that text is read by simpler rules in your browser instead.

Work experience

Internships, jobs, research, on-campus roles — anything with dates and a takeaway. Optional.

Your browser opens the archive itself. Only your positions, projects, skills, certifications and honors are sent — your connections and messages are never read, never uploaded, and nothing is stored. No AI model reads any of it. Everything imported appears below, and on the skills screen, for you to edit or delete.

"Polish with AI" sends that one description, plus the role and employer on the same card, to an AI model and replaces the text with what it writes. It rewords; it cannot add a fact, a tool or a figure your notes don't already contain, and anything it adds anyway is removed before you see it. Nothing is stored, and Undo puts your own words back. One thing worth knowing: a tighter rewrite is a shorter one, and whatever it trims is no longer there for the resume drafter to use.

Projects

Class projects, personal builds, hackathons, capstones. Optional.

"Polish with AI" works the same way it does on the previous step: that one description plus the project name and technology go to an AI model, which rewords them and cannot add a fact, a tool or a figure your notes don't already contain. Nothing is stored, and Undo puts your own words back.
